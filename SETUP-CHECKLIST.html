Before you start

Have these to hand

  • An email address for the accounts
  • Your mobile, for verification codes
  • Bank details, so the networks can pay you
  • The dealbot.zip folder, unzipped
Tax

Affiliate commission counts as income. In the UK, if your trading income goes over £1,000 in a tax year (the trading allowance), you need to register for Self Assessment and declare it. Keep the payout statements from eBay and Awin.

Phase 2 · When your eBay and Pinterest keys arrive

Run setup and record the demo

    • python.org/downloads. On the first screen, tick Add python.exe to PATH.
    • It checks your eBay keys work. When it asks whether Pinterest has approved Standard access, answer no.
    • Start a screen recording (Win + Alt + R), double-click demo.bat, and let it post its test pin.
    • The first time, it asks for a sandbox token. Generate one on your app's page at developers.pinterest.com.
    • On your app's page, click Upgrade to Standard and upload the video.
    Standard access request
    This app posts curated UK deals to my own Pinterest boards, around 4 to 6 pins a day. The video shows the app finding a discounted product through eBay's official Browse API, then creating a pin through POST /v5/pins with a title, description, affiliate disclosure (#ad) and destination link. It only accesses my own account.
Phase 3 · After Pinterest approves Standard access

Finish setup and go live

    • It connects your Pinterest account, links your 16 boards, does a test run, and then lists every secret for GitHub.
    • Create an account at github.com and a public repository named YOUR-USERNAME.github.io. Public is needed for the free website; your keys stay hidden in GitHub Secrets.
    • Use GitHub Desktop to upload everything. It skips the private .env file automatically and includes the hidden .github folder.
    • In the repo: Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions → New repository secret. Add one per name.
    • Actions → Deal bot → Run workflow. Tick Dry run first, then run it again without the tick and check the pin appears on Pinterest.

    After this it runs four times a day without you.

Phase 4 · When Awin approves you

Switch on Beauty & Fragrance and Baby & Kids

    • Advertisers → Join programmes. Start with Scentsational Perfumes, The Fragrance Counter, Perfume Market UK, Beauté Boulevard and Mamas & Papas.
    • Search "beauty", "fragrance", "baby", "toys" and "kids" for more. Apply to plenty, as some will decline.
    Message to retailers, if there's a box
    I curate genuine discounts on Pinterest for UK shoppers, with a dedicated board for this category. Pins show the current and previous price, link straight to your product page and are clearly labelled #ad. Organic only, with no brand bidding.
    • Toolbox → Create-a-Feed. One feed with your beauty and fragrance retailers, one with your baby and kids retailers. Format: CSV.
    • Tick these columns:
    Columns to tick
    aw_product_id, product_name, brand_name, merchant_name, search_price, rrp_price, product_price_old, in_stock, currency, aw_deep_link, merchant_image_url, large_image
    • Save them as the secrets AWIN_FEED_BEAUTY and AWIN_FEED_KIDS. The bot picks them up on its next run.
