Privacy policy

This policy covers the Daily Finds UK website, our social media accounts, and the app we use to publish deals to our own Pinterest, Instagram and YouTube accounts. Last updated 8 October 2026.

Who we are

Daily Finds UK is an independent deals curator based in the United Kingdom. Contact: dailyfindsukme21@gmail.com.

Our publishing app

Our app uses the Pinterest API, the Instagram Graph API and the YouTube Data API only to publish posts to our own Daily Finds UK accounts. It does not access, read or post to anyone else's account, and it does not collect data about the people who view our posts.

Information we collect

  • This website has no sign-up, no forms and no analytics tracking. We do not collect personal information.
  • The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.
  • Our app keeps a list of products it has already posted. This list contains no personal information.

Affiliate links and cookies

When you click a deal, you go to the shop through the eBay Partner Network or Awin. They and the shop may set cookies to record that a sale came from us. Their own privacy policies apply to that.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about visitors, there is usually nothing for us to share, correct or delete. If you have any question, email us and we will reply within 30 days. You can also contact the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
